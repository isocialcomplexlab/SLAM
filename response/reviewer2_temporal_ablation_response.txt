Reviewer 2 — Temporal sequence-length ablation

Comment:
The manuscript used a fixed sequence of three consecutive distance matches without providing an ablation or separating descriptor discrimination from temporal post-processing.

Response:
Thank you for this important observation. We revised the methodology and experiments to separate descriptor-level evaluation from temporal verification. Descriptor performance is now reported at L=1, without temporal aggregation. We additionally evaluated strict consecutive-reference windows with L={1,2,3,5}. For every method and L, the operational threshold was calibrated exclusively on the calibration event and then applied unchanged to the held-out test and diagnostic stress events. Windows containing ambiguous geometric pairs were excluded from binary metrics.

On the held-out KITTI 05 test event, the corrected Euclidean descriptor obtained ROC-AUC/AP/F1 values of 0.824498/0.593918/0.491369 at L=1. Although L=2 produced a marginal ROC-AUC increase to 0.824784, AP and F1 decreased to 0.587019 and 0.489590. Performance decreased further for longer windows; at L=5, AP=0.530753 and F1=0.440529. These results show that the original three-match rule is not universally optimal and should not be conflated with descriptor quality.
